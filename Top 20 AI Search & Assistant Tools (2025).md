### **Top 20 AI Search & Assistant Tools (2025)**  

#### **1. [Perplexity AI](https://www.perplexity.ai/)**  
- **Best for**: Research, cited answers, deep topic exploration  

#### **2. [Poe by Quora](https://poe.com/)**  
- **Best for**: Accessing multiple AI models (GPT-4, Claude, Mistral)  

#### **3. [DeepSeek Chat](https://www.deepseek.com/)**  
- **Best for**: Free long-context AI (128K tokens), coding & document analysis  

#### **4. [You.com](https://you.com/)**  
- **Best for**: Privacy-focused, personalized AI search  

#### **5. [Phind](https://www.phind.com/)**

#### **6. [Andi Search](https://andisearch.com/)**  
- **Best for**: Simple, ad-free summarized answers  

#### **7. [Exa (Metaphor)](https://exa.ai/)**

#### **8. [Komo Search](https://komo.ai/)**

#### **9. [Microsoft Copilot](https://copilot.microsoft.com/)**

#### **10. [Blackbox AI](https://www.blackbox.ai/)**  
- **Best for**: Code generation & developer assistance  

#### **11. [Duck AI](https://duck.ai/)**  
- **Best for**: Privacy-first AI answers (beta)  

#### **12. [Gemini (Google AI)](https://gemini.google.com/)**

#### **13. [Claude AI](https://claude.ai/)**

#### **14. [ChatGPT (OpenAI)](https://chat.openai.com/)**  
- **Best for**: General AI chat, GPT-4 (paid) & GPT-3.5 (free)  

#### **15. [Elicit AI](https://elicit.org/)**  
- **Best for**: Academic research & paper summarization  

#### **16. [Inflection Pi](https://inflection.ai/pi)**

#### **17. [Writesonic](https://writesonic.com/)**  
- **Best for**: AI-powered content writing & SEO  

#### **18. [Brave Leo](https://brave.com/leo/)**  
- **Best for**: Private AI chat within Brave browser  

#### **19. [Hugging Face Chat](https://huggingface.co/chat/)**  
- **Best for**: Open-source AI models & experimentation  

#### **20. [NeevaAI](https://neeva.com/)**
